# 🪟 YouTube Fill Window

Makes YouTube videos fill your **browser window** instead of your entire monitor. Built for split-screen and snapped side-by-side multitasking, featuring a **Red Glassmorphism UI**, **Floating Glass Island HUD**, and one-click **Picture-in-Picture**.

---

## ⚡ What's New & Cool

- **Floating Glass Dynamic Island HUD:** An ambient frosted red glass capsule floats over the video when active, giving you smooth controls for Play/Pause, speed cycling (1x–2x), time scrubbing, and Picture-in-Picture — auto-hiding when idle.
- **Picture-in-Picture (PiP) Fast Switch:** Instantly pop the video out into a floating Picture-in-Picture window with one click or by pressing **`P`** on your keyboard!
- **Edge-to-Edge Zoom Mode:** Toggle with **`Z`** or in the HUD to fill out narrow split-screen windows with zero black bars.
- **Red Glassmorphic UI:** Frosted glass popup with glowing crimson ambient lighting.

---

## ⌨️ Quick Keyboard Shortcuts

- **`W`** or **`Alt + Shift + F`**: Instantly toggle Window Fill.
- **`P`**: Toggle Picture-in-Picture (PiP).
- **`Z`**: Toggle between Fit (letterbox) and Edge-to-Edge Zoom (no black bars).
- **`Esc`**: Exit Fill Window anytime.

---

## 🚀 How to Install (Chrome, Brave, Edge, Opera)

1. Open your browser and go to:
   - **Chrome:** `chrome://extensions`
   - **Brave:** `brave://extensions`
   - **Edge:** `edge://extensions`
2. Turn **ON** the **Developer mode** toggle in the top-right corner.
3. Click **Load unpacked** (top-left) and select this `youtube-fill-window` folder.

*(Tip: Click the puzzle piece 🧩 in your browser toolbar to pin the extension icon!)*

---

## 🎬 How to Use

- **In the player:** Click the **window icon** next to YouTube's fullscreen button, or press **`W`**.
- **Floating Island HUD:** Move your mouse over the video while in Fill Window mode to reveal the floating glass island.
- **Picture-in-Picture:** Click the **PiP** button in the floating HUD, inside the popup, or press **`P`**.
- **Auto-enable:** Check *"Turn on automatically"* in the popup if you want videos to fill by default.

---

> **Note:** If you had YouTube already open before installing or updating, refresh that tab once (**Ctrl+R** or **Cmd+R**).
