# YouTube Fill Window

Makes YouTube videos fill your **browser window** instead of your entire monitor. Perfect for split-screen and watching videos side-by-side with your notes or work.

---

## How to Install (Chrome, Brave, Edge)

1. Open your browser and go to:
   - **Chrome:** `chrome://extensions`
   - **Brave:** `brave://extensions`
   - **Edge:** `edge://extensions`
2. Turn **ON** the **Developer mode** switch in the top-right corner.
3. Click **Load unpacked** (top-left) and select this `youtube-fill-window` folder.

*(Tip: Click the puzzle piece 🧩 in your browser toolbar to pin the extension icon!)*

---

## How to Use

- **In the player:** Click the new **window icon** next to YouTube's fullscreen button.
- **Keyboard shortcut:** Press **`Alt + Shift + F`** to toggle.
- **To exit:** Press **`Esc`** on your keyboard or click the button again.
- **Auto-enable:** Click the extension icon in your toolbar and check *"Turn on automatically for every video"* if you want it on by default.

---

> **Note:** If you already had YouTube open before installing, simply refresh that tab once (**Ctrl+R** or **Cmd+R**).
